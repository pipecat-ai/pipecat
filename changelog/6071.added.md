- Added `VolumeFrame` and `VolumeGainFrame` to control the volume of the bot's audio. `VolumeFrame` sets the output transport's volume, and `VolumeGainFrame` sets a gain on top of it for some audio, for example `VolumeGainFrame(0.5)` before a `TTSSpeakFrame` and `VolumeGainFrame(1.0)` after it to play that utterance at half volume. Both take effect in order with the audio, and an interruption doesn't drop them.
