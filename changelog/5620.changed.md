- ⚠️ **Behavior change:** Pipecat now supports the openai 3 SDK, and the `openai` dependency is widened to `>=1.74.0,<4`, so a fresh resolve picks openai 3. It builds its HTTP clients on `httpx2` instead of `httpx`, and TLS certificates then verify against the operating system trust store rather than `certifi`. Minimal container images without system CA certificates, and environments behind a TLS-inspecting proxy, may need `SSL_CERT_FILE` or `SSL_CERT_DIR` pointed at a CA bundle. Pin `openai<3` to stay on the previous HTTP stack.

    A `Timeout` passed to a service's `http_client` — `OpenAITTSService` and the Whisper-based STT services — must come from the HTTP client family the installed SDK uses: `httpx2` on openai 3, `httpx` before it.
