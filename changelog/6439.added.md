- Added universal names to expressive conversation (alpha): `UniversalDelivery` and `UniversalSound`, listed in `UNIVERSAL_DELIVERY` and `UNIVERSAL_SOUNDS`, name each delivery and sound the TTS services with an expression adapter perform, the same on every service. A universal name in a tag, such as `<<warm>>` or `[[laugh]]`, is sent to each service by its own name, such as ElevenLabs' `warmly` or Cartesia's `laughter`, while the spoken words' `delivery`, the sounds reported to clients and the context keep it as written. `ExpressionCapabilities.service_names` maps the names that differ, filled in for Cartesia, ElevenLabs, Fish Audio and MiniMax, and `ExpressionCapabilities.delivery_name()` and `sound_name()` give the name a service performs.
