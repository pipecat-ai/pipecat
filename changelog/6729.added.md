- Eval transports now advertise their configured audio directions before `bot-ready`. `EvalSessionParams.audio_support_check` (default `off`) checks them on audio runs: `when_advertised` refuses a target that advertises a direction the run uses as off and runs a target that advertises nothing as unverified, and `required` refuses both. `EvalScriptResult.audio_support` and `EvalSimulationResult.audio_support` record the outcome.
