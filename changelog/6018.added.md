- Added Pulse 2.0 support to `SmallestSTTService` via `SmallestSTTModel.PULSE_2` (`model="pulse-2"`): an English-only streaming model with built-in end-of-turn detection (`endpointing` has no effect on it), plus per-utterance `emotion` and `gender` on final transcripts, controlled by the new `emotion_detection`/`gender_detection` settings (on by default).
