- OpenAI and Gemini defer only a lone internal transition until speech finishes; ordinary, mixed, multiple-tool batches, and transitions with TTS disabled execute immediately. Interrupted speech and shutdown discard deferred transitions. Response generations and interruption IDs keep late tool parsing and delayed playback notifications from affecting a fresh response.
- Added `on_function_calls_prepared` so applications can accept ordinary tool work before a sibling transition shuts down its pipeline worker.
- A transition arriving after its response's speech has already stopped executes immediately; subsequent text or resumed playback starts a new wait.
