- A finished session's pipeline is no longer kept in memory for up to `turn_end_timeout_secs` (2.5 s by default) after it ends. `TurnTrackingObserver`, which `PipelineWorker` adds by default (`enable_turn_tracking=True`), could leave its turn-end timer scheduled when the pipeline was torn down before the observer saw the final `EndFrame` or `CancelFrame`, and the timer's callback held the pipeline until it fired. A garbage collection or memory release right after the session could not free it.
