- Fixed the MoQ transport silently dropping an outbound audio frame (logged as `Error writing audio frame: unsupported operand type(s) for +=: 'NoneType' and 'float'`) when an interruption reset the audio pacing clock while a paced `publish_audio` write slept. A reset landing mid-sleep now re-anchors the encoder epoch and pacing clock the same way a fresh write does, and a transport teardown landing in the same sleep (the peer leaving while the bot speaks with a full buffer) drops the write quietly instead of erroring on the closed producer.
