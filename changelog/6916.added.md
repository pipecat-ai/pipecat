- Added `text_transforms` to `STTService`, with `add_text_transformer()` and `remove_text_transformer()`, as on `TTSService` and `LLMTextProcessor`. Each transcript is rewritten before the service pushes it, so the context, observers such as the RTVI client transcript, and the STT tracing span see only the rewritten text: for example, to redact card numbers a caller reads out. A transform sees a final transcript as `TranscriptionType.FINAL` and an interim one as `TranscriptionType.INTERIM`. A transcription whose text a transform changed loses its `result`.
