- Fixed `strip_complete_markup()` (and `split_markup_runs()`) removing the words between a `<` and a later `>` used as comparisons: markup is now a tag starting with a letter or `/!/`?`, or a whitespace-free digit-leading token (numeric pause tags such as Rime's `<500.0>` or an expression adapter's `<400ms>`), so text like "Is 3 < 5 and 7 > 2?" or "3<5 and 7>2" keeps its comparison operators instead of showing as "Is 3  2?". Emoticons like `<3` are unaffected.
