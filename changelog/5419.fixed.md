Fixed `TextSegmentMap` rejecting word-timestamp tokens that carry leading punctuation (e.g. a quote following an asterisk or newline run): the matcher skipped the segment's leading punctuation but never the incoming word's, so the hop returned NO_MATCH, the slot force-completed, and the rest of the reply was dropped from word-timestamp-driven output. The word is now also retried with its leading non-alphanumeric run stripped, as the last strategy before rejection.
