- Added `UserTurnStrategiesParams` (in `pipecat.turns.user_turn_strategies`) to choose how user turn strategies combine. Pass it as `user_turn_strategies_params` to `LLMUserAggregatorParams`, `UserTurnProcessor` or `UserTurnController`. `start_mode` and `stop_mode` take a `StrategyMode`: with `FIRST` the first strategy to decide wins, and with `ALL` every strategy has to agree. `start_mode` defaults to `FIRST` and `stop_mode` to `ALL`. `start_merge_mode` and `stop_merge_mode` take a `MergeMode`: `REPLACE`, the default, uses the bot's strategies instead of the ones a service recommends, and `EXTEND` adds them to the service's.

  ```python
  # Filter incomplete turns on top of a service's stop strategies.
  user_params = LLMUserAggregatorParams(
      user_turn_strategies=FilterIncompleteUserTurnStrategies(),
      user_turn_strategies_params=UserTurnStrategiesParams(
          stop_merge_mode=MergeMode.EXTEND,
      ),
  )
  ```
