- When filtering incomplete turns, a turn the LLM marks ◐ or ○ now stays open until the marker strategy's own timeout (`UserTurnCompletionConfig.incomplete_short_timeout` or `incomplete_long_timeout`) re-prompts the user. Before, `user_turn_stop_timeout` ended the turn first, a moment before the re-prompt, and fired `on_user_turn_stop_timeout`; it now only ends turns no stop strategy will end.
