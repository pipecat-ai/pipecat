- Fixed TTS word tracking falling out of step on markdown-heavy replies. Most word-timestamp events were dropped with "Dropping word ... not recognised by any slot" warnings, and the rest of the reply arrived in large chunks, so word highlighting stalled and then jumped ahead. Two token shapes triggered it: the period Cartesia adds to the last token of a line (`images:.`, `---.`), and a symbol a provider reports differently from the text (ElevenLabs reports `→` as `-`) followed by more symbols such as `###` or `**`.
