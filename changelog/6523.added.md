- Added `SpeechSegmentBuffer` in `pipecat.audio.speech_segment_buffer`, which keeps one stretch of the user's speech with the audio just before it. Between stretches of speech it keeps the last `pre_speech_secs`, 1 s by default, so a segment starts before the VAD reports speech. From `start()` to `stop()` it keeps everything, or the last `max_secs`, and `stop()` returns the segment. `SegmentedSTTService` keeps its segments in one.
