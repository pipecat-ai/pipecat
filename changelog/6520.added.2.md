- Added `BackgroundWorkStartedFrame` and `BackgroundWorkFinishedFrame`, broadcast to bookend background work that doesn't block the conversation. `LLMService` broadcasts them around an asynchronous function call (`cancel_on_interruption=False`), and `BackendConnector` around the work an `LLMWithBackend` backend has in flight. A component that dispatches other such work, like a job to another worker, should broadcast the same pair, keyed by `work_id`.
