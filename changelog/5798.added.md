- Added `TTSService.pronounce_ipa()` and `pronounce_arpabet()`, which build a text transform from a word-to-phonemes mapping. Each matched word is replaced with the service's `format_pronunciation()` output, so one pronunciation works with any service that supports its alphabet; words a service cannot use are reported once and spoken as written. Cartesia renders IPA as inline phoneme blocks, ElevenLabs as SSML phoneme tags in IPA or CMU Arpabet, Eleven v3 and Inworld as IPA between slashes, and Deepgram Aura-2 as an inline pronunciation object. Deepgram Flux has no pronunciation markup, so its words are spoken as written. Parsing and normalization shared by the formatters live in `pipecat.utils.text.phonemes`.
