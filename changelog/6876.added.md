- Added a `with_diarization` argument to `SarvamRealtimeSTTService`. When Sarvam has enabled the diarization beta for your account, `saaras:v4` returns each word's time and speaker in the frame's `result`, final transcripts are split into one `TranscriptionFrame` per speaker with `speaker` set, and the new `speaker_format` setting (`{speaker}`, `{speaker_number}`, `{text}`) can label the text.
