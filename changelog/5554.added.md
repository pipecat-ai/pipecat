- Added `apply_text_normalization` to `ElevenLabsDialogueTTSService.Settings`, which controls whether ElevenLabs converts numbers, dates and currencies to words before speaking them. `"on"` always converts, so `07/03/2026` is read as "July third, twenty twenty-six"; `"off"` skips the conversion; `"auto"` lets ElevenLabs decide. Left unset, ElevenLabs' default, `"auto"`, applies.
