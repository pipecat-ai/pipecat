- Added expressive conversation (alpha), turned on with `PipelineParams(enable_expressive_conversation=True)`: TTS services perform expression tags the LLM writes into its reply, instead of speaking them. The tag syntax and these APIs are likely to change between releases.
  - `<<name>>` sets the delivery of the words that follow, such as `<<excited>>`, until the next delivery tag or the end of the response, and `<<neutral>>` returns to the voice's baseline. `[[sound]]` is a sound at one point, such as `[[laughs]]`, `[[pause 500]]` is a pause in milliseconds, and `^^word^^` stresses a word. `<<slow: A B 7>>` speaks just those words in that delivery, on top of the delivery around them. `<<slow>>` and `<<fast>>` are deliveries every service that performs them names the same, for saying something carefully, such as a code, or in passing; Cartesia and MiniMax change speed only between sentences.
  - Each TTS service performs what its expression adapter supports and leaves out the rest, with a warning. Adapters subclass `BaseExpressionAdapter` and live in `pipecat.adapters.tts`, beside the LLM adapters. Adapters ship for `CartesiaTTSService` and `CartesiaHttpTTSService`, `ElevenLabsDialogueTTSService`, `ElevenLabsHttpTTSService` on Eleven v3 and v4 models, `FishAudioTTSService` on S2 models, and `MiniMaxHttpTTSService` on speech-2.8 models. A service broadcasts what it can perform at start in a `TTSMetadataFrame`.
  - The context records the reply as the LLM wrote it, tags included, and clients see the words without the tags. From a service that reports word timestamps, clients receive each word's `delivery` on `bot-output`, and each sound and pause where it is performed, as a `bot-output` segment of the `expression_sound` or `expression_pause` type. A service without word timestamps reports each sentence in the delivery it starts in, after its sounds and pauses. Expression sounds and pauses are sent to clients of RTVI protocol 2.3.0 or later, the protocol version now.
  - Examples for each provider are in `examples/expressive-conversation/`.
