- Added expressive conversation (alpha), turned on with `PipelineParams(enable_expressive_conversation=True)`: TTS services perform expression tags the LLM writes into its reply, instead of speaking them. The tag syntax and these APIs are likely to change between releases.
  - `<<mode>>` sets the delivery of the words that follow, such as `<<excited>>`, until the next mode tag or the end of the response, and `<<neutral>>` returns to the voice's baseline. `[[event]]` is a sound at one point, such as `[[laughs]]`, `[[pause 500]]` is a pause in milliseconds, and `^^word^^` stresses a word.
  - Each TTS service performs what its expression adapter supports and leaves out the rest, with a warning. Adapters subclass `BaseExpressionAdapter` and live in `pipecat.adapters.tts`, beside the LLM adapters. Adapters ship for `CartesiaTTSService` and `CartesiaHttpTTSService`, `ElevenLabsDialogueTTSService`, `ElevenLabsHttpTTSService` on Eleven v3 and v4 models, `FishAudioTTSService` on S2 models, and `MiniMaxHttpTTSService` on speech-2.8 models. A service broadcasts what it can perform at start in a `TTSMetadataFrame`.
  - The context records the reply as the LLM wrote it, tags included, and clients see the words without the tags. From a service that reports word timestamps, clients receive each word's `modes` on `bot-output`, and each event and pause where it is performed, as a `bot-output` segment of the `expression_event` or `expression_pause` type. A service without word timestamps reports each sentence in the modes it starts in, after its events and pauses.
  - Examples for each provider are in `examples/expressive-conversation/`.
