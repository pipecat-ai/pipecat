- Added `SIPTransport`, a native SIP transport built on
  [baresip-python](https://pypi.org/project/baresip-python/). It registers a
  SIP account (or dials through a registration-less trunk), answers dial-in
  and places dial-out calls with the Daily dial vocabulary
  (`on_dialin_ready`, `on_dialin_connected`, `on_dialout_answered`,
  `start_dialout()`, `send_dtmf()`, `sip_refer()`, ...), and supports DTMF in
  both directions, hold/resume, and VP8 video. The development runner gains
  `-t sip` (account from `SIP_USER`/`SIP_PASS`/`SIP_DOMAIN`/`SIP_TRANSPORT`).
  Install with `pipecat-ai[sip]`, or `pipecat-ai[sip-video]` for video calls.
