- Added LiveKit as a transport option in the development runner: `python bot.py -t livekit`, a `GET /livekit` convenience route that mints a room and redirects to a hosted LiveKit client, and `POST /start` support (`"transport": "livekit"`, with an optional `livekitRoomName`). Requires `LIVEKIT_URL`, `LIVEKIT_API_KEY`, and `LIVEKIT_API_SECRET` to be configured on the server.
