- Fixed `DeepgramFluxSTTService` and `DeepgramFluxSageMakerSTTService` losing the session to a connection setting Flux refuses. Flux rejects the whole connection for more than 100 keyterms, a keyterm over 100 characters, or an `eager_eot_threshold` outside 0.3 to 0.9 or above `eot_threshold` (which Flux treats as 0.7 when the connection sets none), and that rejection leaves the service unusable for the rest of the call. Those values are now truncated or dropped with a warning, so the connection is made with the rest of the settings intact.
