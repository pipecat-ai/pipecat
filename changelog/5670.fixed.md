- Local STT and TTS services now reject a missing or blank model or voice at startup with a "must be specified" error. Piper, Pocket TTS, Kokoro, and XTTS check the voice in the constructor (Pocket TTS before loading its model, and Kokoro also against the loaded voices file), and Whisper, Whisper MLX, and Moonshine check the model. Previously a blank value, which is what a copied `.env.example` produces, reached the underlying library and failed with an unrelated message, sometimes only on the first utterance. XTTS also reports an unknown studio speaker as an error frame instead of raising a `KeyError`.
