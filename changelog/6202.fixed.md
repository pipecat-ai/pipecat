- Fixed `SileroVADAnalyzer` sometimes reporting that the user stopped speaking partway through a word, which could cut off the end of a transcript or close the turn on incomplete audio. The analyzer reset the model's state every five seconds of wall-clock time, and a reset during speech could drop the model's confidence below the threshold. Resets now happen only during clear non-speech and are timed by the audio processed, so they never land on speech and the result no longer depends on wall-clock timing.
