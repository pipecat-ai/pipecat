- Pipelines now start without waiting for the STT service to connect. Fourteen services — AssemblyAI, AWS Transcribe, Cartesia, ElevenLabs Realtime, Gladia, Gradium, Mistral, OpenAI Realtime, Sarvam, Smallest, Soniox, Speechmatics, Together and xAI — established their connection inside `start()`, adding the handshake to pipeline startup. They now connect in the background and hold frames until the connection can carry them, saving between 76 ms and 1185 ms depending on the service.

  Because `start()` runs serially down the pipeline, the saving compounds: a `ServiceSwitcher` holding several STT services no longer pays their handshakes one after another.
