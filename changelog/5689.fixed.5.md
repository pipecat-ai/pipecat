- Fixed `BackendLLMWorker` carrying on with work its requester had let go of. When `OpenAILiveLLMService` abandons a client delegation, because it timed out or the conversation was reset, the worker's model kept running and any tool call it was waiting on kept waiting, and their output could be reported under a later delegation. The worker now interrupts its pipeline and cancels its function calls in flight when its `attach` job is cancelled, and `OpenAILiveLLMService` detaches and attaches again in both cases.
