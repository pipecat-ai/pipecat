- Fixed `BackendLLMWorker` carrying on after the job it was working on was cancelled. When `OpenAILiveLLMService` cancels a client delegation, because it timed out or the session ended, the worker's model kept running and any tool call it was waiting on kept waiting, and their output could land in the next delegation the worker took. The worker now interrupts its pipeline when its job is cancelled, so the run ends and the next delegation starts clean.
