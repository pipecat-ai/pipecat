- `TavusTransport` and `TavusVideoService` now set the Tavus replica's facial emotion from the delivery the bot speaks in, when expressive conversation is enabled (`PipelineParams(enable_expressive_conversation=True)`). They convert each `TTSTextFrame.delivery` to a `TavusEmotion` and send `conversation.set_emotion` when it changes, as the frame's words are heard. `TAVUS_DELIVERY_EMOTIONS` maps Pipecat's core deliveries, such as `warm` to `happiness`, and a universal delivery for each other emotion, such as `angry` to `anger`; any other mood shows `neutral`. A delivery the expression policy offers that the replica has no emotion for is logged as a warning at startup.
