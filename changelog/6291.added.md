- `TavusTransport` and `TavusVideoService` now set the Tavus replica's facial emotion from the delivery the bot speaks in, when expressive conversation is enabled (`PipelineParams(enable_expressive_conversation=True)`). They convert each `TTSTextFrame.delivery` to a `TavusEmotion` and send `conversation.set_emotion` when it changes, as the frame's words are heard. `TAVUS_DELIVERY_EMOTIONS` lists the universal deliveries Tavus has an emotion for, such as `happy` for `happiness`, to offer in an `ExpressionPolicy`; any other mood shows `neutral` and logs a warning.
