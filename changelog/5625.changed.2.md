- ⚠️ **Behavior change:** the Deepgram Flux services no longer push their eager end-of-turn transcript as an `InterimTranscriptionFrame`. That transcript is a prediction the service may withdraw, and it now travels as an `EagerEndOfTurnTranscriptionFrame` instead, so it is no longer surfaced to clients as partial user speech. Flux emits no interim transcriptions as a result; subscribe to `on_update` for incremental transcript text.
