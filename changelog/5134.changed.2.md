- STT and TTS services now stop working once the provider has rejected their configuration — a bad API key, an unknown model or voice — instead of retrying it for every chunk of audio or piece of text.

    Previously a rejected API key on a service that connects on demand produced a connection attempt and an `ErrorFrame` several times a second for as long as the pipeline ran. `STTService` and `TTSService` now skip transcription and synthesis while the service is `MISCONFIGURED`, and websocket services stop reconnecting.

    Services whose credentials are signed or resolved per connection — `AWSTranscribeSTTService` and `NvidiaSageMakerTTSService` — treat a rejected credential as recoverable, since reconnecting is what refreshes it.

    Pair this with `PipelineWorker(on_configuration_error=...)` or an `on_pipeline_configuration_error` handler to decide what the bot should do about it.
