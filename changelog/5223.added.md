- Added an `extra_headers` argument to `CartesiaSTTService` and `CartesiaTTSService`, matching `CartesiaTurnsSTTService`. The headers are sent with the websocket handshake, so deployments can supply their own authentication or routing headers. `CartesiaSTTService`'s `base_url` now also accepts a URL carrying a scheme (`ws://localhost:8000`) rather than only a bare host, so the connection can be made over plain `ws` against a local or proxied endpoint instead of always `wss`.
