- Realtime (speech-to-speech) LLM services are now traced as one `llm_response` span per reply, nested inside the `turn` span, running from the end of the user's speech to the end of the reply (or the user's speech onset when interrupted). The user's transcript (`input`), the reply text (`output`), both modalities, `gen_ai.output.type`, TTFB (`metrics.ttfb` and `gen_ai.response.time_to_first_chunk`), token usage including the audio breakdown, and `llm.interrupted` are attributes on it. Each function call becomes an `llm_tool_call` span (`gen_ai.operation.name=execute_tool`, `gen_ai.tool.name`, `gen_ai.tool.call.id`, `gen_ai.tool.call.arguments`, `gen_ai.tool.call.result`). This replaces the per-handler `llm_response` spans, which measured a handler and lasted under a millisecond; the `stt` spans; OpenAI Realtime's `llm_request` span; and Gemini Live's `llm_tool_call`/`llm_tool_result` spans with their `tool.*` attributes. `llm_setup` is unchanged. Applies to `GeminiLiveLLMService`, `GeminiLiveVertexLLMService`, `OpenAIRealtimeLLMService`, `AzureRealtimeLLMService`, `AzureVoiceLiveLLMService`, `AWSNovaSonicLLMService`, `GrokRealtimeLLMService`, `InworldRealtimeLLMService` and `UltravoxRealtimeLLMService`. `OpenAILiveLLMService` is not traced this way.
