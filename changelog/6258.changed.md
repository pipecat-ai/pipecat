- ⚠️ Realtime (speech-to-speech) LLM services emit different tracing spans, and `llm_response` has a new meaning: it covers a whole reply instead of one message handler. Applies to `GeminiLiveLLMService`, `GeminiLiveVertexLLMService`, `OpenAIRealtimeLLMService`, `AzureRealtimeLLMService`, `AzureVoiceLiveLLMService`, `AWSNovaSonicLLMService`, `GrokRealtimeLLMService`, `InworldRealtimeLLMService` and `UltravoxRealtimeLLMService`; `OpenAILiveLLMService` is not traced this way.
  - `llm_response`: one span per reply, nested inside the `turn` span, from the end of the user's speech to the end of the reply (or the user's speech onset when interrupted). Attributes: `input` (the user's transcript), `output` (the reply text), `input_modality`, `output_modality`, `gen_ai.output.type`, TTFB (`metrics.ttfb` and `gen_ai.response.time_to_first_chunk`), token usage including the audio breakdown, and `llm.interrupted`.
  - `llm_tool_call`: one span per function call, beside `llm_response`, with `gen_ai.operation.name=execute_tool`, `gen_ai.tool.name`, `gen_ai.tool.call.id`, `gen_ai.tool.call.arguments` and `gen_ai.tool.call.result`. These replace the `tool.*` attributes of Gemini Live's `llm_tool_call` span.
  - `stt`: removed.
  - `llm_request` (OpenAI Realtime) and `llm_tool_result` (Gemini Live): removed.
  - `llm_setup`: unchanged.
