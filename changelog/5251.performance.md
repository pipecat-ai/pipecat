- Pipelines now start without waiting for the STT service to connect. Thirteen services — AssemblyAI, AWS Transcribe, Cartesia, ElevenLabs Realtime, Gladia, Gradium, Mistral, OpenAI Realtime, Sarvam, Smallest, Speechmatics, Together and xAI — established their connection inside `start()`, which added the handshake to pipeline startup for every processor behind them. They now connect in the background and hold audio until ready, saving between 76 ms and 1511 ms depending on the service.

  Because `start()` runs serially down the pipeline, the saving compounds: a `ServiceSwitcher` holding several STT services no longer pays their handshakes one after another.
