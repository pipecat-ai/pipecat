- ⚠️ A SIP account that registers now sends its calls through its registrar, on the connection the registration keeps alive, so a far-end hang-up reaches the bot on a long call. `SIPConnection(registrar=...)` defaults to the account's own domain. The far end sees the registrar's address, not the bot's, and a registrar that does not relay calls to other domains refuses them; use a connection with `reg_interval=0`, addressed at that domain, to call it directly.
