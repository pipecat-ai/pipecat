- Added perception to expressive conversation (alpha): with `PipelineParams(enable_expressive_conversation=True)`, the LLM reads how the user spoke, as reported by a perception producer such as an STT service or a processor after it.
  - `TranscriptionFrame`, `InterimTranscriptionFrame` and `EagerTranscriptionFrame` carry the reading in a new `perception` field: `PerceptionSignal`s, each read as the likeliest labels with how sure the reading is, such as a tone of `frustrated 0.86`, or as a value. An STT service that perceives sets it on the transcriptions it pushes, and a processor after the STT can set it on the transcriptions it passes along. A reading of an interim transcription is provisional. A reading of an eager transcription is written into the user message that an early reply, with `enable_eager_end_of_turn`, is generated from. The producer declares the signals it reports in a `PerceptionMetadataFrame` at start.
  - The user aggregator writes the turn's final reading at the head of its user message, such as `([tone frustrated=0.86, neutral=0.10]) Can you tell me where my order is?`, and the LLM service's system instruction explains how to read it: what the confidences mean, that a reading below 0.5 is a guess, and each signal the producer declares. `ExpressionPolicy` guidance can say what to do with a reading, such as "If the user sounds frustrated at 0.7 or above, apologize once".
  - Apps get the turn's final reading in `UserTurnStoppedMessage.perception`, on the user aggregator's `on_user_turn_stopped` event. Processors after the user aggregator get the reading written into the context in `LLMContextUserTurnFrame.perception`. Clients get the reading in the `perception` field of the transcription's `user-input` message, which waits for a processor after the STT to read it.
