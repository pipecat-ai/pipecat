- Added `LLMInstructorMetadataFrame`, broadcast at pipeline start by a processor that instructs the LLM: by contributing standing guidance for the LLM's system instruction, by prompting the model itself, or both. Its two fields, each optional, auto-configure the `LLMService` in the pipeline:
  - `instruction`: additional system instructions, which the service composes onto the end of its own (as `append_system_instruction` does) and keeps across context resets.
  - `silent_mark`: the mark the instructor's prompts offer the model for saying nothing, which the service honors from then on. A reply that opens with it is dropped before anything downstream or any observer sees it, nothing is recorded in the context, and the decision is reported as an `LLMMarkerResponseFrame` of kind `silent`, which the RTVI observer sends as `bot-llm-marker` to clients that asked for marker reports. A scripted eval asserts it with `event: llm_marker` and `marker: silent`.

  `WaitReassurance` broadcasts one.
