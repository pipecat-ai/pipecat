- Added `LLM_SILENT_MARK` (`∅`), the mark a reply opens with when the model decides to say nothing. `LLMService` reads it (`read_silent_mark`, `None` to read none): the reply is dropped before anything downstream or any observer sees it, nothing is recorded in the context, and the decision is reported as an `LLMMarkerResponseFrame` of kind `silent`, which the RTVI observer sends as `bot-llm-marker` to clients that asked for marker reports. A scripted eval asserts it with `event: llm_marker` and `marker: silent`.
