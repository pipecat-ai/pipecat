- Added `LLMInstructorMetadataFrame`, broadcast at pipeline start by a processor that instructs the LLM: standing guidance for the LLM's system instruction, a silent mark its prompts offer the model, or both. `LLMService` composes the instruction in, as `append_system_instruction` does, and honors the mark once announced: a reply that opens with it is dropped before anything downstream or any observer sees it, nothing is recorded in the context, and the decision is reported as an `LLMMarkerResponseFrame` of kind `silent`, which the RTVI observer sends as `bot-llm-marker` to clients that asked for marker reports. A scripted eval asserts it with `event: llm_marker` and `marker: silent`.
