- ⚠️ In realtime mode, `LLMUserAggregator` passes an `LLMMessagesAppendFrame` on to the realtime service, and `LLMAssistantAggregator` adds its messages to the context. A processor between the two aggregators now sees the `LLMMessagesAppendFrame`, where it used to see an `LLMContextFrame` travelling downstream with the messages already in the context.
