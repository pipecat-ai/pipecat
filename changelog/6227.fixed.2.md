- Fixed `RTVIObserver` holding `bot-output` for text the TTS service left out of speech until the bot next started speaking, so a text-only reply, such as one from a bot with an `LLMTextProcessor`, could wait for the next spoken reply or never go out. The TTS service marks the text it leaves out with `skip_tts`, and the observer sends that text right away, unless text queued before it is still waiting for the bot to speak. Other text, such as a speech-to-speech service's transcript, still waits for the bot to start speaking.
