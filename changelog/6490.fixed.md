- Fixed WebSocket services leaving a provider connection open when their disconnect was cancelled before the connection closed, which could happen when a `CancelFrame` arrived during shutdown. The connection kept answering keepalive pings until the process exited, so providers that limit concurrent sessions, such as Deepgram Flux, could start refusing new connections (HTTP 429). A service that clears or replaces its connection while it is still open now aborts it, which covers every service built on `WebsocketService`.
