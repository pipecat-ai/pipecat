- `SimliVideoService` now sends the bot's audio to Simli as it arrives, whatever the face. With `is_trinity_avatar=True` it used to hold back up to 4 seconds of TTS audio at the start and after each interruption, and play it with `sendImmediate`. That pre-buffer is removed, and every interruption now clears Simli's buffer. A user who starts speaking without interrupting the bot, as with `OpenAILiveLLMService`, no longer clears it, so the avatar keeps talking when the bot does.
