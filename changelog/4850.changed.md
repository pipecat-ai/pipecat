- Frame-push TRACE logging in `FrameProcessor` is now opt-in via the `PIPECAT_TRACE_FRAMES` environment variable. The two `logger.trace()` calls on the per-frame push path built their f-string arguments on every frame hop (thousands of times per second per live pipeline) even when the TRACE level was filtered out; guarding them measurably reduces event-loop load under many concurrent pipelines.
