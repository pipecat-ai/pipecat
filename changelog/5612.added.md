- Added `SpeakingObserver`, which reports a conversation's speaking lifecycle through `on_speech_event`: `user_speech_started` / `user_speech_stopped` as the detector heard them, `user_turn_started` / `user_turn_stopped` as the turn strategy ruled on them, `bot_speech_started` / `bot_speech_stopped`, and `interruption`. Speech is timed to where it began and ended rather than to where the detector confirmed it, and a moment that closes a stretch of speech names its `started_at`, so an interval reads whole from one record.
