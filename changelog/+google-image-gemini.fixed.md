- Fixed `GoogleImageGenService` failing on every request with "This method is only supported in Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform mode". Google shut down the Imagen models it defaulted to; the service now generates images with Gemini image models through `generate_content`, defaults to `gemini-3.1-flash-image` with a new `aspect_ratio` setting (default `"1:1"`, matching the square images Imagen produced), and emits RGB frames.
