- Fixed `GeminiLiveLLMService` leaving thinking tokens out of `completion_tokens`, and reporting a `total_tokens` that could also leave them out. Gemini Live reports thinking tokens in `thoughts_token_count`, apart from `response_token_count`, and its own `total_token_count` sometimes omits them. `completion_tokens` now includes them, and `total_tokens` is `prompt_tokens` plus `completion_tokens`.
