- `AnthropicLLMService` now logs a warning naming Claude's `stop_reason` when a response ends for a notable reason, for both streamed responses and `run_inference()`: refused by a safety classifier (with the refusal category), or truncated at `max_tokens` or the context window. Previously these ended the turn, or the one-shot inference, with little or no text and no indication why. Whatever text did arrive is still passed downstream or returned, and responses ending normally are unaffected.
