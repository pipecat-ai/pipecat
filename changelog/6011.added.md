- Added a keepalive to `WebsocketTTSService` for custom websocket TTS services whose provider closes idle connections. Override `_send_keepalive()` to send the provider's keepalive message, and call `_create_keepalive_task(interval)` / `_cancel_keepalive_task()` from `_connect()` / `_disconnect()`. `_send_keepalive()` is called only while the websocket is open, and an exception it raises is logged.
