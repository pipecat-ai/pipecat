- `LiveKitOutputTransport` now sends every button of a multi-digit `OutputDTMFFrame` or `OutputDTMFUrgentFrame` instead of only the first one. A tone that cannot be sent stops the rest of the sequence and is reported with an `ErrorFrame` instead of only being logged; `LiveKitTransportClient.send_dtmf()` now raises in that case (not connected, invalid digit or publish failure).
