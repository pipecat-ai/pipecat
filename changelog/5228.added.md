- Added `ResponseFrame` for scheduling assistant-initiated speech from any source — a timer, a webhook, another worker. Queue one wrapping an `LLMMessagesAppendFrame` (or a `TTSSpeakFrame` to say something verbatim) and the configured strategy releases it at the next opening. New `on_response_deferred` and `on_response_released` events on the assistant aggregator observe the lifecycle, and `BaseResponseStrategy` (`pipecat.turns.response`) is the extension point for custom timing policies. See `examples/features/features-delayed-responses.py`.
