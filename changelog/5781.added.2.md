- `GeminiLiveLLMService` honors the `interaction_status` on `server_content`: while it is `IN_PROGRESS` the bot turn is held open (`TTSStoppedFrame` and `LLMFullResponseEndFrame` are deferred), so a reply spanning several `turn_complete` chunks arrives as one assistant turn — closed after 30 seconds if the session never goes idle. Models without the status behave as before. The `google` extra now requires `google-genai>=2.18.0`, the first version delivering the field; older SDKs log a warning naming the upgrade.
