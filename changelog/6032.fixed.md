- Fixed websocket STT services staying deaf for the whole session when their first connect failed. `WebsocketSTTService` now retries a connect that left no websocket, up to 3 times with backoff once audio arrives, then reports a permanent error so a `ServiceSwitcher` can fail over. This affects services that never retried a failed connect, such as `AssemblyAISTTService`, `SonioxSTTService` and `SarvamRealtimeSTTService`.
