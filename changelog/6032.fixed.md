- Fixed websocket STT services staying usable but transcribing nothing for the rest of the session when their first connect failed. A failed connect is now retried like a dropped connection, with backoff, and reported as permanent once the attempts run out, so a `ServiceSwitcher` can fail over. This applies to `AssemblyAISTTService`, `AWSTranscribeSTTService`, `GladiaSTTService`, `GradiumSTTService`, `MetaSTTService`, `OpenAIRealtimeSTTService`, `SarvamRealtimeSTTService`, `SonioxSTTService`, `TogetherSTTService` and `XAISTTService`.
