- The eval judge can decide with TypeSafe judgments instead of an LLM: `service: typesafe` in a scenario's `judge.eval:` block builds a `TypeSafeEvalJudge` (`pipecat.evals.typesafe_judge`). A scripted `eval:` expectation becomes one yes/no/continue `Choice`, and a simulation run one request with a `Noul` for the goal and one per bot turn per metric. Verdicts carry their probabilities as the reason.
