- The eval judge can decide with TypeSafe judgments instead of an LLM: `service: typesafe` in a scenario's `judge.eval:` block builds a `TypeSafeEvalJudge` (`pipecat.evals.typesafe_judge`). A scripted `eval:` expectation becomes two `Noul`s in one request (is the answer finished, does it satisfy the criterion), and a simulation run one request with a `Noul` for the goal and one per bot turn per metric, where a probability too close to the threshold is no verdict. Verdicts carry their probabilities as the reason.
