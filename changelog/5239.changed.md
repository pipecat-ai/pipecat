- The `aic` extra now requires `aic-sdk~=3.0`. The SDK's 3.0 release made all
  audio APIs mono-only and moved voice activity detection into a dedicated
  `Vad` type, so `AICFilter` and `AICQuailVADAnalyzer` process 1-D mono blocks
  and the analyzer drives a `Vad` rather than an enhancement `Processor`.
  `aic-sdk` 2.5.x is no longer supported.
