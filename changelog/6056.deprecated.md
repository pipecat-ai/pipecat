- Deprecated calling `SmallWebRTCTransport.capture_participant_video()` without a participant id, as `capture_participant_video(video_source="camera")` or `capture_participant_video("camera")`. Pass the peer's id first, as on other transports: `capture_participant_video(client_id, framerate, video_source)`. Calls without it keep passing on every frame unless a `framerate` is given.
