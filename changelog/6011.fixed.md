- Fixed websocket TTS services losing their keepalive partway through a session. In `AsyncAITTSService`, `ElevenLabsTTSService`, `ElevenLabsDialogueTTSService`, `InworldTTSService`, `NeuphonicTTSService`, `SarvamTTSService`, `SmallestTTSService` and `SonioxTTSService`, a single failed keepalive stopped all later ones, so the provider closed the connection after every idle period and the service reconnected each time. A failed keepalive is now logged and the next one is sent on schedule. `NeuphonicTTSService` also no longer sends keepalives on a closed connection.
