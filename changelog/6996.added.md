- Added `mode="manual"` to `AssemblyAISTTService.Settings`. AssemblyAI then ends no turns on its own: each VAD stop sends a ForceEndpoint and gets one final transcript for the whole turn, so pauses shorter than the VAD's `stop_secs` (inside a phone number, for example) no longer split it into several finals. It needs `vad_force_turn_endpoint=True` (the default) and the `assemblyai-stt-3-7-realtime-agent` model, and can't be combined with `speaker_labels`.
  ```python
  stt = AssemblyAISTTService(
      api_key=os.environ["ASSEMBLYAI_API_KEY"],
      settings=AssemblyAISTTService.Settings(mode="manual"),
  )
  ```
