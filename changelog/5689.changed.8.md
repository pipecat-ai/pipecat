- The eval harness (`pipecat.evals`) reports a function call made outside the bot's pipeline and reported into it (an `ExternalFunctionCall*Frame`, such as a backend's call) as a `function_call` / `function_call_stopped` event like the pipeline's own, and keeps a function-call event that arrives while a turn waits on another expectation, so a later call expectation in the same turn can still claim it.
