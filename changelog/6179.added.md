- `JevClassifier` can see images: pass `images=` to `ask()`, `yes_no()`, `choice()` or `score()`. Jev takes PNG and JPEG images, 256 to 1568 pixels on each side, and rejects images outside those limits rather than resizing them. A WebP image raises `ClassifierError` before any request. TypeSafe offers images as a preview, so the limits may change between Jev releases.
