- Websocket services now stop reconnecting once the service is known to be misconfigured, instead of retrying credentials the provider has already rejected.

    Reconnection also tracks the service's `ServiceStatus`: `READY` once a connection is established, `DEGRADED` while retrying, and `UNAVAILABLE` after the attempts are exhausted. Errors reported during reconnection now carry the exception that caused them, so they can be classified.

    This applies to services that opt into error classification; the rest reconnect exactly as before.
