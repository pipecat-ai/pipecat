- Fixed the WebSocket transports treating an audio frame as unsent whenever the serializer emitted no payload for it. A serializer that resamples through a stream resampler buffers audio across calls and emits it in a later one, so on a pipeline whose output sample rate differs from the wire rate most frames took that path. Those frames are now paced and pushed downstream like any other, so the output queue drains at playback speed again and `BotStoppedSpeakingFrame` and the `EndFrame` behind it are no longer reached early, which matters most where the serializer hangs up the call on the `EndFrame`.
