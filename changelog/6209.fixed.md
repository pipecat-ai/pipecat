- `AssemblyAISTTService`, `AWSTranscribeSTTService`, `GladiaSTTService`, `MetaSTTService`, `OpenAIRealtimeSTTService`, `SonioxSTTService`, `TogetherSTTService` and `XAISTTService` retry a failed first connection instead of transcribing nothing for the rest of the session. A failed connect is retried with backoff like a dropped connection; if every attempt fails, the service reports a permanent error, so a `ServiceSwitcher` using `ServiceSwitcherStrategyFailover` switches to its next service.
