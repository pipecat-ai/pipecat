- Fixed an async function call's result going unreported when the conversation moved on while the call was still running. A tool registered with `cancel_on_interruption=False` keeps running after the LLM's turn ends, so by the time its result arrives the user has often changed the subject — and the LLM would answer the new topic without ever mentioning the result. A short result is stated outright, a long one named with an offer of the details.
