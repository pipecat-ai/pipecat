- Fixed `expand_units` expanding only the part of a rate before the slash: `100 km/h` read "100 kilometers/h" and `9.8 m/s` read "9.8 meters/s". A unit followed by a time unit after a slash (`/h`, `/hr`, `/s`, `/sec`, `/min`, ...) now reads as a rate, e.g. "100 kilometers per hour" and "100 megabytes per second".
