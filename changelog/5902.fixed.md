- `BaseOutputTransport` now measures its bot-stopped-speaking fallback, used when TTS audio arrives without a later `TTSStoppedFrame`, from the last audio frame it wrote rather than from the last frame of any kind on its queue. Heartbeats and other non-audio frames used to keep restarting the window, so with `enable_heartbeats=True` the fallback never fired and a function result that arrived while the bot was marked speaking never re-ran the LLM.
