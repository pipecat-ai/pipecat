- Added `NvidiaOmniLLMService` for NVIDIA Nemotron Omni models, which take speech, images, and video as well as text. With `"audio"` in `Settings.input_modalities`, it buffers the user's speech between VAD boundaries and transcribes and answers it in a single request, replacing the STT service; `Settings.emit_transcriptions` also pushes the user's transcript upstream for the context. Audio and video files in the context, from `LLMContext.create_file_message()` or `create_audio_message()`, are sent in the form Omni reads by the new `NvidiaOmniLLMAdapter`. `run_multimodal_inference()` runs a one-off request outside the conversation, for example to describe a video clip, with optional streaming callbacks for text and reasoning. See `examples/voice/voice-nvidia-omni.py`.
