- ⚠️ A custom stop strategy that decides from the turn's inference, for example one that called `trigger_user_turn_finalized()` next to `deferred()` strategies, must now return True from `BaseUserTurnStopStrategy.waits_for_inference`. Otherwise the turn controller treats it as a strategy that listens to the user: in a `StrategyMode.ALL` list the inference doesn't run until `user_turn_stop_timeout` ends the turn, and in a `StrategyMode.FIRST` list the turn ends without waiting for it.
