- Added `pairing_instruction` to `BackendLLMWorker` and `frontend_pairing_instruction` to `BackendConnector`: the text each appends to its model's system instruction about working as one half of the pair, in place of `BACKEND_PAIRING_INSTRUCTION` and `FRONTEND_PAIRING_INSTRUCTION`, for an app that would rather say in its own words when and how to delegate, that to the user there is one assistant, what to do with the backend's messages, and, on the backend, that the user hears what it marks with `>>` and how to take requests that arrive while it works. The request strategies take their own texts as arguments too: `frontend_instruction` (how the request is worded, placed where the frontend's pairing instruction has `{strategy}`), `tool_description` and `request_instruction` (what is placed after the request for the backend).
