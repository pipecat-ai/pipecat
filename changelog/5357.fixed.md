- Fixed word-level TTS tracking breaking when a TTS service normalizes typographic punctuation in its word-timestamp events, e.g. reporting `don't` for a `don’t` it was sent, or the reverse, and likewise for curly quotes and en/em dashes. The reported word failed to match the text being spoken, so the rest of the sentence lost its per-word `TTSTextFrame` and `AggregatedTextProgressFrame` and reached the conversation context as a single block at the end. Word matching now folds those variants in either direction, alongside case and accents, while the text attached to the context keeps the original typography.
