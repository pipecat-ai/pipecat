- Added finish reason logging to OpenAI-compatible LLM services (`OpenAILLMService` and the services built on `BaseOpenAILLMService`), including `run_inference()`. A response that ends for a reason other than a normal stop, such as `length` (truncated at the output token limit) or `content_filter` (withheld by the provider), now logs a warning naming the `finish_reason`. Previously these ended the turn with little or no text and no indication why. Whatever text did arrive is still passed downstream, and responses ending normally are unaffected.
