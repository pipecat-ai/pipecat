- RTVI now reports bot backchannels to clients of protocol 2.2.0 or later. Text of type `backchannel`, such as a `BotBackchannelFrame`'s, is sent as `bot-output` with `text_type` (and the deprecated `aggregated_by`) set to `"backchannel"`, and as `bot-tts-text`. Add `"backchannel"` to `RTVIObserverParams.skip_text_types` to keep it out of both messages for every client.
