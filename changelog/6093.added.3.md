- RTVI now reports backchannels. Text of type `backchannel`, such as a `BotBackchannelFrame`'s, is sent as `bot-output` with that text type and as `bot-tts-text`, to clients of protocol 2.2.0 or later; add `"backchannel"` to `RTVIObserverParams.skip_text_types` to keep it from every client. The observer sends a `user-backchannel` message for each `UserBackchannelFrame`, which `RTVIObserverParams.user_backchannel_enabled` turns off.
