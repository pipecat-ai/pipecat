- ⚠️ `WakePhraseUserTurnStartStrategy` now starts turns on its own: once awake, it starts a turn when the user speaks or a final transcription arrives. Use it as the only start strategy, `start=[WakePhraseUserTurnStartStrategy(...)]`. In a `StrategyMode.FIRST` list, the start strategies after it no longer decide once it's awake. To combine it with others, such as `MinWordsUserTurnStartStrategy`, use `start_mode=StrategyMode.ALL`.
