- Updated `sentencex` to 1.0.34, which fixes a crash in sentence aggregation when a one-letter word is followed by a non-breaking space (or other non-ASCII whitespace) and a period. The crash raised `pyo3_runtime.PanicException`, which `except Exception` does not catch. The update also stops French, German, Spanish, Italian and Portuguese text from splitting after title abbreviations such as `M.` in `M. Dupont`.
