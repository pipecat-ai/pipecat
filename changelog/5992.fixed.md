- Fixed TTS services that could go silent for the rest of the session with `buffer size must be a multiple of element size` when metrics were enabled. A provider can split its audio stream mid-sample, and the odd-length audio frames crashed the TTS playback task. Every TTS service now emits audio frames that hold whole 16-bit samples, carrying a split sample over to the next frame, and the time-to-first-audio scan ignores a trailing partial sample.
