- Simulated scenarios in `pipecat.evals`: a scenario file with `persona:` and `goal:` lets an LLM play the caller, and a judge decides `success:` over the whole conversation, with the bot's tool calls as evidence, and scores per-reply `metrics:` (a judged `criterion` with an optional `min_quality`, or a `measure` of `turns`, `duration`, `words`, or `latency` with a range). A simulation's `runs` must all pass. `pipecat eval run` and a suite manifest's `scenarios:` take either kind, and `pipecat eval suite -k` filters by kind.
- `RTVIClientTransport` and `RTVIClientSerializer`: a Pipecat pipeline as an RTVI client. The eval harness runs on them, with STT, TTS, and recording inside its own pipeline and each suite run in its own subprocess.
