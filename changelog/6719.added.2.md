- Added `PipecatVoiceSource` and `PipecatVoiceReader` in `pipecat.expressive.processors.voice_reader`, Pipecat Voice Reader as one of a `PerceptionReader`'s sources and as a reader of its own. Each time the user stops speaking, it hears their speech and reports their `tone` of voice, `arousal`, `laughter`, `speech_rate` and `sarcasm`, and `sad` with `sad=True`. In one `PerceptionReader` it goes beside `PipecatToneSource`, which reads the words and reports a `sarcasm` of its own, with the voice's asked under another name: see `examples/expressive-conversation/expressive-conversation-voice-reader.py`.
