- A service that fails to connect while the pipeline is setting up is left unable to do its job, so a `ServiceSwitcher` moves off it. Setting up is not attempted again, so the service is finished rather than having a bad moment; the switcher settles on its backup before the pipeline starts, and every frame reaches a service that connected. The failure was previously read as temporary, leaving the switcher on the service that never connected and sending it the work.
