- `LLMWorker` and `LLMContextWorker` now forward extra keyword arguments to `PipelineWorker`, so an LLM worker can take `enable_tracing`, `conversation_id`, `additional_span_attributes`, `observers` and the other `PipelineWorker` options. `params` and `idle_timeout_secs` keep their defaults (metrics and usage metrics on, no idle timeout) unless given.
