- Fixed `InworldTTSService` dropping an entire utterance when synthesis is slow. Closing the context at end of turn let the server's `contextClosed` ack overtake the first `audioChunk`, tearing down the local audio context so every late chunk was rejected (`unable to append audio to context`) — 20–40s of dead air on a call. The end-of-turn close is now deferred until the first chunk arrives, and a context with a pending close stays recreatable so audio that outlives the idle timeout still plays.
