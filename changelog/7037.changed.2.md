- ⚠️ A SIP call that receives no audio for 30 seconds is now closed as dead, with the close reason `"rtp timeout"` (reported as `"rtp stream error"` before). Before, the check was off unless `rtp_timeout` or `SIP_RTP_TIMEOUT` was set, so a hang-up that never reached the bot left the session running. Held and send-only calls are not timed. Set `SIPConnection(rtp_timeout=...)` or `SIP_RTP_TIMEOUT` to change the limit; 0 turns the check off.
