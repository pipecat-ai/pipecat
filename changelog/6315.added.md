- Added the LLM's side of expressive conversation (alpha): with `PipelineParams(enable_expressive_conversation=True)`, an LLM service teaches itself the expression tags the TTS service it feeds can perform, in its system instruction, so a prompt doesn't need to describe them. It reads them from the `TTSMetadataFrame` that TTS service broadcasts.
  - `PipelineParams(expression_policy=...)` takes an `ExpressionPolicy`, which every LLM service in the pipeline is taught, each one in an `LLMSwitcher` included. Its `delivery` and `sounds` narrow the tags offered, and its `guidance` lines say when to use them: presets such as `ExpressionGuidance.MATCH_STYLE`, which answers in the user's mood and energy, and `ExpressionGuidance.SLOW_FOR_DETAILS`, which says just a code or a number slowly, in a span such as `<<slow: A B 7>>`, and the developer's own, joined in order. Tags in the policy the TTS service can't perform are left out, with a warning.
  - The instruction follows the active TTS service. A `ServiceSwitcher` switch, or a settings change such as a new model that changes what a TTS service performs, broadcasts its `TTSMetadataFrame` again, and the LLM's instruction is rebuilt from it.
  - `expression_instructions()` returns the instruction for a service's capabilities and a policy, to read or to build a prompt of one's own, and `expression_policy_warning()` what that policy asks for that the service can't perform.
