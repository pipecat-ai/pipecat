- `AWSBedrockLLMService` now logs a warning naming Bedrock's `stopReason` when a response ends for a notable reason — truncated at the output token limit or the context window, or withheld by a guardrail or content filter — in both streaming and `run_inference()`. Previously these ended the turn with little or no text and no indication why. Whatever text did arrive is still passed downstream, and responses ending normally are unaffected.
