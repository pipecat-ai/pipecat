- A provider that reports a punctuation mark as its own word-timestamp entry no longer costs a turn its unspoken tail. `advance_by_alnums` pulls trailing punctuation along with the word before it, so the mark sat behind the llm cursor while nothing had spoken it: ending a frame there discarded the remaining text instead of recording it, and the mark's own event then spent a further character on the next word. Most visible for languages reported one character at a time, where every mark is affected, but not specific to them — `"Yeah"` then `","` then `"I"` lost a character too.
