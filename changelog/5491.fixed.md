- Fixed a per-tool `timeout_secs` (and the global `function_call_timeout_secs`) being disarmed by an intermediate result. An async tool that reported progress with `FunctionCallResultProperties(is_final=False)` cancelled its own deadline on the first update, so a handler that then hung ran unbounded. Only a final result disarms the deadline now, and an intermediate one doesn't restart it either: the budget covers the handler's whole execution. A tool that streams progress and legitimately runs longer than its deadline needs a larger `timeout_secs`, since it is now cancelled at the deadline like any other call.
