- `EvalScenario`, `EvalSession`, `EvalResult`, `EvalTurn`, `EvalTurnResult`, and `EvalTurnProgress` are now `EvalScriptScenario`, `EvalScriptSession`, `EvalScriptResult`, `EvalScriptTurn`, `EvalScriptTurnResult`, and `EvalScriptTurnProgress`, the scripted kind of scenario. The old names remain as aliases until 2.0.0.
