- `EvalJudge` decides every verdict with a `BaseClassifier`, which its constructor takes: an `LLMClassifier` over the LLM a `judge.eval:` block names, as before, or a `JevClassifier` for `service: typesafe`. A classifier gives no reasons, so the judge asks an LLM of its own, the `explainer:` block's or the judging LLM, for the reason behind every `no` and every verdict below `explain_below`; the classifier's verdict stands either way, and `explainer: false` judges without reasons.
