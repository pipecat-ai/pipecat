- Fixed `GeminiSTTService` finalizing utterances early. The service sent `audio_stream_end` on every `VADUserStoppedSpeakingFrame`, so a caller who paused longer than the VAD's `stop_secs` mid-utterance had that utterance cut into separate transcripts, with words dropped at the seam. Added `vad_force_finalize` (default `True`, preserving current behavior); set it to `False` to let the model's own endpointing decide where utterances end.
