- `OpenAIResponsesLLMService` and `OpenAIResponsesHttpLLMService` now send a default reasoning effort, when `reasoning` isn't configured, only to models where it measurably shortens responses: `minimal` to `gpt-5`, `gpt-5-mini` and `gpt-5-nano`, and `none` to `gpt-6-luna`. Every other model runs at OpenAI's default; the later mainline models already answer conversational turns without reasoning. The services log the effort the first time they apply it to a model.
