- Fixed a reply that skips TTS, such as one to text sent with `audio_response: false`, never reaching clients as `bot-output`, so clients that show the bot's text from `bot-output` didn't show it. The TTS service now cuts the LLM's text into sentences and pushes them as unspoken output, which `RTVIObserver` sends as `bot-output` with `will_be_spoken: false`. Processors after the TTS service get these `AggregatedTextFrame`s for such a reply instead of its `LLMTextFrame`s.
