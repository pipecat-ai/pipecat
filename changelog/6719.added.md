- Added `PipecatVoiceClassifier` in `pipecat.classifiers.voice.classifier`, a classifier that runs [Pipecat Voice Reader](https://huggingface.co/pipecat-ai/voice-reader) on the CPU and hears how a clip of the user's speech sounds. It answers the questions built into the model, listed in `PipecatVoiceClassifier.questions`: the speaker's `tone` of voice as a choice, `sarcasm`, `laughter` and `sad` as yes/no questions, and `arousal`, `upset` and `speech_rate` as scores. Any other question raises a `ClassifierError`. Every call takes one clip of 0.8 to 8 seconds, and `sarcasm_threshold` is the probability from which the model's release counts a turn as sarcastic. The model comes in two sizes (`variant="base"` or `"tiny"`) and downloads from Hugging Face on first use. Install it with `pipecat-ai[expressive]`.
