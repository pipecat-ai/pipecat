- `CartesiaSTTService`, `ElevenLabsRealtimeSTTService` and `SmallestSTTService` no longer stop transcribing after a dropped connection is reconnected. Audio arriving during the reconnect could open a second connection alongside the automatic one, leaving the service reading one socket while sending audio to the other, so no transcripts arrived until the provider closed the idle socket. The automatic reconnect is now the only one, audio arriving while the connection is down is dropped, and a failed first connection is retried with the same backoff.
