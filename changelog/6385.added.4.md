- Added `ExpressionGuidance.BREATHE` to expressive conversation (alpha), a preset for an occasional audible breath before a long or difficult sentence, at most once a reply. `PipecatToneTagger` writes breaths only with it, drawing each as a light breath, a deep breath or an exhale at the shares people take them in, and naming each kind as the TTS service performs it: on ElevenLabs, `[quick breath]`, `[deep breath]` and `[exhales softly]`.
