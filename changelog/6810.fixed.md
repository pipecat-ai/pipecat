- A simulation's `measure: latency` metric now times each end by its own side's modality: it starts at the persona's send for a text caller, or the bot noticing the persona stop for a spoken one, and ends at the reply's first token for a text judge, or the bot starting to speak for an audio judge. A text caller with an audio judge previously timed nothing, and an audio caller with a text judge included the caller's own speech. A reply that opens with a function call is timed to its first text token, not to the empty LLM response that made the call. A turn left unanswered when the run ends by silence or its time limit now counts with its wait up to the end, so such a run no longer passes a latency bound on the replies it did get.
