- SIP examples in `examples/sip/`: a `dial-in/` bot that answers calls and a
  `dial-out/` bot that places them, both with numbered call-progress
  milestones at INFO, a `provision.py` CLI that provisions Daily SIP clients
  for softphone calls and a Twilio Elastic SIP trunk for real PSTN calls over
  REST, a REST auto-caller that needs no phone, and a `README.md` that
  teaches the telephony topology.
