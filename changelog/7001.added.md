- Added `enable_reconnect` to `SonioxSTTService.Settings`. When enabled, a dropped connection resumes the same Soniox stream instead of starting a new one, so the transcript carries on and the audio sent around the drop is not lost. The service pings the stream to notice a dead connection, holds what it sends until Soniox confirms receiving it, and resends what Soniox missed. It starts a new stream if Soniox refuses the reconnect. Off by default, because Soniox bills the time a dropped stream waits for a reconnect (up to its 20 s idle timeout). See https://docs.soniox.com/stt/rt/stream-reconnect.
