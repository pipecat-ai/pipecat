- Added audio to classifiers: `ask()`, `yes_no()`, `choice()` and `score()` take a clip of audio as `audio=`, a `ClassifierAudio` of 16-bit mono PCM, the way they take `images=`. A classifier that can hear says so with `supports_audio`, and declares the clips it accepts with `min_audio_secs`, `max_audio_secs` and `audio_sample_rate`; the base class checks a clip's length and resamples it before `_ask()` gets it. A classifier that can't hear refuses a clip with a `ClassifierError`, and its `_ask()` needs no `audio` parameter.
