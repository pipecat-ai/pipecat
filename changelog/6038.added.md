- Added `LiveKitParams.audio_in_user_tracks`, matching `DailyParams.audio_in_user_tracks`. Set it to `False` to mix all participants' audio into one real-time stream of `InputAudioRawFrame`s. With the default, `True`, each participant's audio enters the pipeline as its own stream of `UserAudioRawFrame`s, so a bot with one STT in a room where several participants publish a microphone receives audio faster than real time: its transcripts fall further and further behind and it answers earlier questions.
