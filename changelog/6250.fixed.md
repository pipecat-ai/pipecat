- `GenesysAudioHookSerializer` now follows the AudioHook protocol for pausing. It pauses when Genesys sends `paused` and resumes on `resumed`, dropping audio in both directions while paused, and fires the new `on_paused` and `on_resumed` events. Previously it waited for a `pause` message that Genesys never sends and logged Genesys's `paused` and `resumed` as unknown, so a hold went unnoticed.
