- Added `PipecatToneClassifier` in `pipecat.classifiers.tone.classifier`, a classifier that runs Pipecat Tone's reader model on the CPU. It answers the questions built into the model, listed in `PipecatToneClassifier.questions`: the turn's `emotion` and `move` as choices, and `sarcasm` and `backchannel` (whether the turn is only a listening sound, such as "mhm") as yes/no questions. The questions take their options from the model, which `setup()` loads, and any other question raises a `ClassifierError`. The state is the turn's text, or `{"user": ..., "assistant": ...}` to read it with the bot's words before it.
