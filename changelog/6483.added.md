- Added `clone_strength` to `ElevenLabsDialogueTTSService.Settings`. The Eleven v4 models read it on the Text-to-Dialogue WebSocket: it controls how closely a Professional Voice Clone fine-tuned for a v4 model follows its samples (ElevenLabs recommends 0 for most use cases), and on the Text-to-Dialogue endpoints it decides whether a v4 PVC runs as a PVC or an IVC. It is sent only to `eleven_v4` models and dropped with a warning otherwise.
