- Added `max_retries` and `request_timeout_secs` to `BaseOpenAILLMService` (and the OpenAI-compatible services built on it) and to `OpenAIResponsesHttpLLMService` and `OpenAIResponsesLLMService`. They set the OpenAI client's automatic retry count and per-request timeout, so a real-time app can surface a failed or stalled request at once instead of waiting through the SDK's retries and 600 second read timeout.
