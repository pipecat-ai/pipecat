- `AnthropicLLMService` now defaults to `claude-sonnet-5`, Anthropic's replacement for the now-legacy `claude-sonnet-4-6`. Sonnet 5 rejects `temperature`, `top_p` and `top_k` with a 400, so drop them from your settings if you set any; it also thinks adaptively by default, and that thinking counts against `max_tokens`. For the lowest latency, pass `extra={"output_config": {"effort": "low"}}`.
