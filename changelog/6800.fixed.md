- `RTVIProcessor` now answers a malformed `raw-audio` or `raw-audio-batch` message, such as one missing `sampleRate` or with audio that isn't base64, with an `error-response`, so the client knows its audio wasn't received. Previously the error was only logged, and a bad chunk in a `raw-audio-batch` dropped the chunks after it while keeping the ones before; now a batch with a bad chunk pushes none of its audio.
