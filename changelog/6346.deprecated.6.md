- Deprecated `BaseUserTurnStartStrategy.trigger_user_turn_started()` and `BaseUserTurnStopStrategy.trigger_user_turn_stopped()`. Use `report_user_turn_started()` and `report_user_turn_stopped()` instead: a strategy reports its decision, and the turn controller decides when the turn starts and ends. A subclass that overrides the old method keeps getting the reports until 2.0.0.
