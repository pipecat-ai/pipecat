- Fixed `SarvamSTTService` never reconnecting after its websocket dropped. It now reconnects with a bounded backoff, retries a failed first connect, fires `on_connected`, `on_disconnected` and `on_connection_error`, and reports itself unusable once it gives up so a `ServiceSwitcher` can take over. A connection Sarvam closes for rejected settings isn't retried, and speech a dropped connection cuts short ends with it.
