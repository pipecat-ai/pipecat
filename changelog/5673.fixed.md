- Fixed `XAISTTService` emitting each utterance's text twice. xAI sends a chunk final for every locked segment and then an utterance final that restates the whole utterance, and both were pushed as `TranscriptionFrame`, so LLM context aggregators and other consumers that accumulate finals saw the text duplicated. Chunk finals are now pushed as `InterimTranscriptionFrame` and only the utterance final becomes a `TranscriptionFrame`, always with `finalized=True`.
