- Speech start and stop are now detected the same way regardless of how much audio a transport delivers per chunk. `VADAnalyzer` checked `start_secs` and `stop_secs` once per call instead of once per analysis frame, so a chunk carrying several analysis frames could cross a threshold and fall back out of it before the check ran, and the start or the end of the turn went unreported. WebSocket transports, which take whatever chunk size the client sends, are the most exposed.
