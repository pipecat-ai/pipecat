- InworldTTSService now keeps generation timing per contextId instead of in service-global scalars. Previously a late timestampInfo or flushCompleted from an interrupted context advanced the shared offset, giving the next context's words a future PTS (observed ~10.5 s of skew) so its timestamped text and aggregation stayed queued long after the audio finished. Events for an interrupted or closed context are now dropped rather than replayed.
