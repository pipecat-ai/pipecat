- `GoogleLLMService` and `GoogleVertexLLMService` now send a default thinking config, when `thinking` isn't configured, only to models where it measurably shortens responses: a zero `thinking_budget` to `gemini-2.5-flash`, `thinking_level` `minimal` to `gemini-3-flash-preview`, `gemini-3.5-flash` and `gemini-3.6-flash`, and `low` to `gemini-3.7-flash` and `gemini-3.8-flash`. Every other model runs at Google's default, including the Flash-Lite models, which answer without thinking, and any Flash model not on the list. The services log the config the first time they apply it to a model.
