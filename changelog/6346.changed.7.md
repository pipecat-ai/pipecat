- ⚠️ Turning on turn-completion markers with only the deprecated `filter_incomplete_user_turns` LLM setting no longer re-prompts a user who goes quiet after ◐ or ○, since `LLMTurnCompletionUserTurnStopStrategy` now does the re-prompting. Use `user_turn_strategies=FilterIncompleteUserTurnStrategies()` instead.
