- `ElevenLabsTTSService` now warns at construction when given an Eleven v3 or v4 model. The text-to-speech WebSocket endpoint serves no v3/v4 model, so these settings previously failed later with an opaque 400 at the WebSocket handshake that left the processor unusable; the warning points at `ElevenLabsDialogueTTSService` (real-time) and `ElevenLabsHttpTTSService` (HTTP streaming), which do serve them.
