- Added `max_consecutive_zero_audio_contexts` to `TTSService`. A provider can accept every request and answer with silence — an unknown voice ID, say — without ever reporting an error, leaving the bot mute with nothing in the logs to explain it. After this many consecutive TTS contexts complete without producing audio, the service reports a permanent error, stops being given work, and the pipeline worker applies its `ProcessorUnusablePolicy` (a `ServiceSwitcher` fails over to another provider). Defaults to 3; set it to 0 to leave silent contexts unchecked.
