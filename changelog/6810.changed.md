- With an audio judge, a simulation's `measure: latency` metric now ends when the bot starts speaking (`bot_started_speaking`), not at its first spoken-word report (`tts_response`). How much lower audio-judged latencies come out depends on the bot's TTS: about 0.1 s with one that reports word timestamps, such as Cartesia, and up to the length of the first sentence with an HTTP TTS that doesn't, such as Deepgram's or OpenAI's, so bounds tuned on the old values may need tightening. For a spoken caller, latency starts when the bot reports the caller stopped, so it leaves out the bot's end-of-turn wait and isn't voice-to-voice latency. The metric's reason now names both ends.
