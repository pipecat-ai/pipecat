- Fixed websocket TTS services mishandling the rest of a bot turn after reconnecting mid-turn. The remaining sentences reused the turn's context ID on the new connection, which the provider had never seen, so `InworldTTSService` could drop them without an error and `ElevenLabsTTSService` could speak them without the configured voice settings. Open audio contexts now close when the service replaces its connection, and the rest of the turn continues under a new context ID.
