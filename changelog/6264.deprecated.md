- Deprecated `text_filters` on `TTSService`, along with `BaseTextFilter` and
  `MarkdownTextFilter`. Use `text_transforms` instead: `strip_markdown` from
  `pipecat.utils.text.transforms` replaces `MarkdownTextFilter`, and a transform
  registered on `LLMTextProcessor` rewrites the context as well as the speech. In place of
  `filter_code` and `filter_tables`, give the block its own text type with
  `PatternPairAggregator` and list it in the TTS service's `skip_text_types`, which keeps
  it in the context and out of the audio. Will be removed in 2.0.0.
