- Added `GeminiSTTService`, a streaming speech-to-text service using Google's `gemini-3.5-transcribe-live` model over the Gemini Live API. Language is auto-detected by default; `GeminiSTTService.Settings` supports `languages` hints and `adaptation_phrases` to bias recognition toward domain-specific terms. Requires google-genai >= 2.9.0.

    The model detects utterance boundaries itself, and when the pipeline's VAD signals end of speech the service flushes the utterance so the final transcript arrives promptly instead of when the model decides the utterance ended.
