- Fixed a `pipecat eval` scenario stalling until its timeout when a vision bot asked for the user's image before any turn had registered one with `image:` (e.g. a bot that looks at the user while greeting them). The eval transport now answers the request with an error, as the Daily, LiveKit and SmallWebRTC transports do when there's no video, so the bot's function call finishes and the conversation goes on.
