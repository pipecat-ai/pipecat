- Fixed STT services losing their keepalive after a single failed send. `NvidiaSTTService`, `SarvamSTTService` and `GeminiSTTService` (when its keepalive is enabled) stopped sending keepalives for the rest of the session, so an idle connection could be closed by the provider. Websocket STT services such as `CartesiaSTTService`, `GladiaSTTService` and `SonioxSTTService` were affected until their next reconnect. A failed keepalive is now logged and the next one is sent on schedule.
