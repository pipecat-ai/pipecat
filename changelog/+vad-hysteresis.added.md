- Added optional hysteresis to VAD analysis through `VADParams.hold_confidence`. When set on a `VADAnalyzer` such as `SileroVADAnalyzer`, speech starts once confidence reaches `confidence` and volume reaches `min_volume`, and the segment continues while confidence stays at or above `hold_confidence`, including through dips below `confidence`. `min_volume` does not end a segment while hysteresis is on. Leave `hold_confidence` unset to use `confidence` and `min_volume` for the whole segment.
