- Fixed `CartesiaTTSService` silently losing an utterance when the websocket dropped after the transcript was sent but before any audio came back. The service now retains each context's transcripts until its first audio chunk arrives and re-sends them after a reconnect, so the bot speaks the reply instead of staying silent until the next user turn. Contexts that already produced audio are not replayed, to avoid repeating words the user already heard. `CartesiaTTSService` also now emits a `TTSStoppedFrame` for abandoned audio contexts (`push_stop_frames=True`), matching the other websocket TTS services.
