- Deprecated `UIWorker.ui_job_group()`, `UIWorker.start_ui_job_group()`, and `UIJobGroupContext` (all removed in 2.0.0): use `job_group(...)` / `request_job_group(...)` with `ui=UIJobGroupOptions(...)` — client-visible job groups are a `BaseUIWorker` feature now, inherited by `UIWorker`. The deprecated wrappers keep their historical signatures and behavior in the meantime.
