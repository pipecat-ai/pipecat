- Eval scenarios can expect a `metrics` event, the bot's RTVI metrics report, filtered by `metric:` (`ttfb`, `processing`, `tokens`, ...) and by `processor:`, text the measuring processor's name must contain. Every scripted and simulated eval result also carries `bot_metrics`, each metrics report the bot sent during the run with its time, and `pipecat eval suite` writes it to `results.jsonl` for passed and failed runs alike.
