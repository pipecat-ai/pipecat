- Added `ZoomAIRealtimeLLMService`, speech-to-speech conversation with the Zoom AI Voice Agent. Install it with `pipecat-ai[zoom-ai]`. `pipecat init` offers it as "Zoom AI Voice Agent".
  - The Voice Agent detects turns and handles barge-in itself, so the pipeline needs no VAD analyzer.
  - `Settings.speak` sets the voice and `Settings.listen` the custom vocabulary. `Settings.provider` runs the conversation on your own OpenAI or Anthropic account.
