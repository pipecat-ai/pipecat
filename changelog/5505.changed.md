- ⚠️ **Behavior change:** `max_context_tokens` in `LLMAutoContextSummarizationConfig` now bounds the summarizable context instead of the total context: the preserved initial system message no longer counts toward the trigger threshold, since summarization can never compress it. If you tuned `max_context_tokens` against your model's total context window, subtract your system prompt's estimated tokens to keep the same trigger point.
