- Fixed a `DailyTransport` bot that keeps running after its call ends without `leave()` (the bot is ejected, or the room or meeting ends) raising thousands of `ErrorFrame`s per second until the process is killed. A failed app message raised an `ErrorFrame`, which `RTVIProcessor` (added by default with `enable_rtvi=True`) turned into another app message. The transport now reports the disconnect once, through `on_error`, and drops app messages until it joins again.
