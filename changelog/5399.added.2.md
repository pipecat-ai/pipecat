- Added `PipelineWorker(handle_flush_frame=...)`, which says whether a worker answers a flush probe. It defaults to whether the pipeline is unbridged, so a bridged worker leaves the probe to travel on and be completed by the pipeline that owns the transport, which is what makes `flush_pipeline()` on a bridged worker wait for what it produced to be played rather than only for its own queues to empty. A bridged worker with no such peer never completes a flush.
