- In an audio simulation (`judge.modality: audio`), the persona now waits for the bot to finish speaking before it answers. Turn detection on the bot's speech often ended the bot's turn at its first full sentence, so the persona talked over the rest of the reply and the line it heard was dropped from the judged conversation. The bot's turn now ends once the bot reports it stopped speaking and all of its speech is transcribed. When the bot ends the call, the run also waits up to 5 s for its last words to be transcribed, so the bot's closing line is judged. Scripted runs keep turn detection on the bot's audio.
