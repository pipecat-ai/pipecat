- ⚠️ `pipecat eval` requires the STT that transcribes the bot's speech to be a `SegmentedSTTService`, such as the built-in `moonshine` and `whisper` or `DeepgramSegmentedSTTService`. A `judge.transcription.factory`, or a `bot_stt=` passed in code, that isn't a `SegmentedSTTService` now fails the run with a `ValueError` before it connects, since only a segmented STT's transcripts tell the speech the user talked over from the bot's reply; with a streaming STT, that speech could be scored as the reply.
