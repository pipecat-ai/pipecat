- Fixed a pipeline containing a `SyncParallelPipeline` never finishing after an `EndFrame` (for example from `stop_when_done()`) while input audio, RTVI messages or other frames were still queued in its pipelines. The `EndFrame` was dropped and never reached the output transport. When only some of its pipelines had output pending, the `EndFrame` could go out ahead of that output instead. It now goes on after the output of every pipeline.
