- Turn completion in the LLM is now `UserTurnCompletionLLMExtension`, which `FilterIncompleteUserTurnStrategies` installs over a settings update, and which reads the marker opening each response and contributes the turn-completion instructions. `UserTurnCompletionLLMServiceMixin` is removed, and `UserTurnCompletionConfig` and the markers now live in `pipecat.turns.user_turn_completion`. A settings update that sets the deprecated `LLMSettings.filter_incomplete_user_turns` or `user_turn_completion_config` now warns, and installs or configures the extension.
