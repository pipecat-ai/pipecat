- Added an `on_word_timestamps` event to `STTService`, raised by `DeepgramSTTService` with the words of each final transcript as `(word, start, end)` tuples on the input audio's PTS in nanoseconds. Deepgram times words in seconds of audio received on the current connection, so the service records each chunk it actually sends with its input PTS and resets that record on every new connection. Audio that never reached Deepgram (muted, gated, or dropped while reconnecting) and gaps in the input's own media time no longer shift the words after them. Other streaming services can opt in through the new `record_sent_audio()`, `reset_sent_audio()` and `sent_audio_pts()` helpers on `STTService`.
