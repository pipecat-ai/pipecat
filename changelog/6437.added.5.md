- Added `AssemblyAITTSService`, streaming text-to-speech with per-word timings from AssemblyAI's Streaming TTS WebSocket API. Install it with `pipecat-ai[assemblyai]`. `pipecat init` offers it as "AssemblyAI".
  - `Settings.voice` is sent as the API's `voice_id`: any voice by its ID, an AssemblyAI voice by its library slug, such as `aai-jane`, or a cloned voice by the slug the AssemblyAI dashboard shows. It defaults to Audrey, a voice from AssemblyAI's public voice library. `Settings.language` and `Settings.model` are optional.
  - The service connects to the US host; set `url` to `wss://tts.eu.assemblyai.com/v1/ws` to process data in the EU.
  - Interruptions cancel synthesis in flight without reconnecting. The service keeps an idle session open, replaces a session before its one-hour limit, and waits as long as the server asks before reconnecting.
  - See `examples/voice/voice-assemblyai.py`.
