- Added **assistant-initiated responses**: speech the conversation didn't just ask for, such as a background task reporting its result. A *response strategy* decides when that speech is delivered, so it lands in a natural opening instead of on top of whoever is talking. Configure one with `LLMAssistantAggregatorParams(response_strategy=DelayedResponseStrategy())` — the shipped strategy waits until the bot is quiet, the user is quiet, no answer is owed, and a settle window has passed. The feature is opt-in; without a strategy nothing changes. Not supported with realtime (speech-to-speech) services.
