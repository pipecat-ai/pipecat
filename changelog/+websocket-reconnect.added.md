- Added support for replacing a `FastAPIWebsocketTransport`'s WebSocket mid-session, for providers that reconnect a media stream rather than holding a single socket for the whole session. `FastAPIWebsocketTransport.replace_websocket()` moves the running transport onto a new connection, `expect_reconnect()` marks the window where the old socket has closed and its replacement has not yet arrived, `FastAPIWebsocketInputTransport.restart_receive()` rebinds the receive loop, and `TwilioFrameSerializer.set_stream_sid()` retargets outbound messages at the new stream.
