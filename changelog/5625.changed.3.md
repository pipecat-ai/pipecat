- The `on_user_turn_inference_triggered` event emitted by `UserTurnController` and by `BaseUserTurnStopStrategy` now carries a `UserTurnSpeculation | None` as its last argument, naming the speculation an inference answers when the turn it answers hasn't ended yet. A custom stop strategy requests one by passing it to `trigger_user_turn_inference_triggered()`. The events of the same name on `LLMUserAggregator` and `UserTurnProcessor` are unchanged, so handlers registered on those keep their signature.

    ```python
    @controller.event_handler("on_user_turn_inference_triggered")
    async def on_user_turn_inference_triggered(controller, strategy, speculation): ...
    ```
