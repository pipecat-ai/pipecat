- ⚠️ **Breaking change:** Agent STT has no speaker focus, so `SpeechmaticsSTTService` no longer exports `SpeakerFocusMode` or `SpeakerFocusConfig`, and `UpdateParams` and `update_params()` are removed. The `Settings` and `InputParams` fields `focus_speakers`, `ignore_speakers`, `focus_mode`, `speaker_passive_format`, `max_delay`, `end_of_utterance_silence_trigger`, `end_of_utterance_max_delay`, `split_sentences`, `include_results`, and `extra_params` are removed, as are `TurnDetectionMode.FIXED`, `ADAPTIVE`, and `SMART_TURN`. `OperatingPoint` is replaced by `Model`.
