- Fixed a pipeline teardown stall where lifecycle frames (`EndFrame`, `CancelFrame`) could be gated behind a frame-processing pause. When a `FrameProcessor` has paused processing (e.g. a TTS service pausing to avoid audio overlap), lifecycle frames now bypass the pause and are processed immediately. Previously, an `EndFrame` stuck behind the pause could stall pipeline shutdown for up to the pause-watchdog timeout (or deadlock if no watchdog was armed), because a `ParallelPipeline`'s lifecycle-frame synchronization buffers all downstream frames until every branch delivers the lifecycle frame — and the very frames needed to lift the pause (e.g. `BotStoppedSpeakingFrame`) were among those buffered frames.
