- ⚠️ **Breaking change:** `BackendLLMWorker`'s `transform_output` is called as `transform_output(output, is_final=...)`, and `BackendOutput` no longer carries `is_final`: a backend's final output is its job's response, and the outputs before it travel as updates. A transform that takes the output alone is rejected when the worker is constructed, with the new signature in the message, and one that raises fails the delegation instead of leaving it to time out. `BackendReplyStrategy.deliver()` receives `is_final` alongside the output.
