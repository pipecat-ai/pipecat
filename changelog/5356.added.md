- Added a warning when a `thinking_budget` is set on a Gemini 3 model. Gemini 3 takes `thinking_level` instead, and what happens to a `thinking_budget` set on its own varies by model and by backend: it may be honored, silently ignored, or rejected with a `400` that names no field. Use `thinking_level` for Gemini 3 and keep `thinking_budget` for the 2.5 series.
