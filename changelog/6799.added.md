- Added `HaruLLMService` and `HaruLocalLLMService` for [Haru](https://github.com/higurashi-ai/Haru), an open-weight full-duplex speech-to-speech model that listens and speaks at the same time and decides for itself when to talk. `HaruLLMService` connects to a Haru vLLM-Omni server; `HaruLocalLLMService` runs the model in-process, with MLX on Apple silicon (`pipecat-ai[haru-mlx]`) or PyTorch on an NVIDIA GPU (`pipecat-ai[haru-torch]`). See `examples/realtime/realtime-haru.py` and `examples/realtime/realtime-haru-local.py`.
