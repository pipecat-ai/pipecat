- `LLMUserAggregator` and `LLMAssistantAggregator` report backchannels, which stay out of the LLM context and its turn events. `on_user_backchannel` fires with a `UserBackchannelMessage` for each `UserBackchannelFrame` while the user isn't muted, and `on_assistant_backchannel` fires with an `AssistantBackchannelMessage` when the assistant starts saying text of type `backchannel`.
