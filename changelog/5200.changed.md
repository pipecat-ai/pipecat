- `GrokRealtimeLLMService`'s `voice` setting is typed `str` rather than a fixed list of five names, and accepts any built-in Grok voice ID (xAI documents the catalogue at https://docs.x.ai/docs/guides/voice/agent) or a custom ID from the Custom Voices API. Voice IDs are case-insensitive. `GrokVoice` is an alias of `str`.
