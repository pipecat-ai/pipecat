- ⚠️ Several stop strategies in one `UserTurnStrategies.stop` list now all have to agree before a user turn ends, because `UserTurnStrategiesParams.stop_mode` defaults to `StrategyMode.ALL`. For example, `TurnAnalyzerUserTurnStopStrategy` next to `SpeechTimeoutUserTurnStopStrategy` now ends the turn once smart turn says the user is done and the pause has lasted long enough. Pass `stop_mode=StrategyMode.FIRST` to end the turn on the first stop strategy that decides, as before. A stop strategy that waits for the turn's inference, such as `LLMTurnCompletionUserTurnStopStrategy`, still gets its say in a `FIRST` list: the first stop strategy to decide runs the inference, and the turn ends once the strategy waiting for it decides. A list with a single stop strategy behaves as before.
