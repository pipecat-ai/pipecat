- Fixed a session tearing its controllers down twice. `LLMUserAggregator` and `UserTurnProcessor` ran their teardown from the `EndFrame` handler, the `CancelFrame` handler and the guaranteed `cleanup()`, and `BaseInputTransport` stopped its `audio_in_filter` from all three while starting it from one. Anything shared was therefore released once more than it was acquired, so one session ending could release a shared `VADAnalyzer` to nothing while another session was still using it. Controllers now stop their own timers at session end and release what they hold in `cleanup()`, and the input filter starts in `setup()` and stops in `cleanup()`.
