- Added `SarvamRealtimeSTTService` for low-latency streaming speech-to-text with Sarvam's `saaras:v3-realtime` model. Supports server-side endpointing (`endpointing="vad"`) and pipeline-driven endpointing (`endpointing="manual"`), interim and final transcripts, timestamps, in-band configuration updates via `config.update`, and audio-usage metrics sourced from Sarvam's reported duration.
