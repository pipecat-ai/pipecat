- `OpenAILiveLLMService` client delegation now drives its `BackendLLMWorker` through the worker's `attach`, `message` and `cancel` jobs, the same contract `LLMWithBackend` uses: the service attaches once for the session, sends each delegation as a message, and takes the backend's outputs and tool calls from the stream. Delegations still run one at a time, a follow-up starting when the backend reports it has nothing left to do, and `ClientDelegation.timeout_secs` still abandons one the backend takes too long on. `ClientDelegation.backend` now also takes a worker's name, for a backend running in another process on a shared bus.
