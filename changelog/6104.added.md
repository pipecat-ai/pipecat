- `RTVIObserver` can send the raw text of the STT, LLM and TTS as `stt-raw-text`, `llm-raw-text` and `tts-raw-text`, to clients of protocol 2.2.0 or later. They carry the same data as `user-transcription`, `bot-llm-text` and `bot-tts-text`, and are off by default; `RTVIObserverParams.stt_raw_text_enabled`, `llm_raw_text_enabled` and `tts_raw_text_enabled` turn them on.
