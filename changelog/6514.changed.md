- Documented how to prevent internal reasoning leakage on current Claude models (such as `claude-haiku-5-5` and `claude-sonnet-5-5`) in `AnthropicLLMService`. Disabling thinking or using `between_tools` can lead the model to output reasoning directly into spoken reply text ahead of a tool call; setting `effort="low"` in `Settings` enables adaptive thinking at low latency and keeps reasoning in non-spoken thought frames.
