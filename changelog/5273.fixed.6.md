- Fixed `LLMUserAggregator` and `LLMAssistantAggregator` raising `TypeError` on an `LLMSetToolsFrame` carrying provider-native tools, such as `[{"google_search": {}}]`. `LLMSetToolsFrame` documents that form, and speech-to-speech services read it off the frame, but the aggregators tried to fold it into the context first — and the context holds standard tools only. Provider-native tools now pass through the aggregators untouched.
