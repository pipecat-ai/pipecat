- Added expressive conversation (alpha) adapters for five more TTS services, so their LLMs are taught the expression tags they perform. See `examples/expressive-conversation/` for each.
  - `InworldTTSService` and `InworldHttpTTSService` on `inworld-tts-2` perform every universal delivery as a free-text instruction, such as `[calm and slow]`, with `^^emphasis^^` in capitals, Inworld's non-verbal sounds and `<break>` pauses. On `inworld-tts-2-flash`, which ignores instructions, only sounds and pauses are performed.
  - `SonioxTTSService` on `tts-rt-v2` performs deliveries and sounds as Soniox's audio tags, such as `[excited]` and `[laughs]`, with `*emphasis*` and approximate pauses. Its word timestamps report an audio tag as one word and a stressed word without its asterisks.
  - `MistralTTSService` on Voxtral 3 models (`voxtral-mini-tts-3-0`) performs a delivery as one of Mistral's emotions, such as `[emotion=warm]`, slow and fast as `[pace=slow]` and `[pace=fast]`, and sounds such as `[laugh]`. The default model, `voxtral-mini-tts-2603`, performs none.
  - `GeminiTTSService` on Gemini 3.8 models with the Gemini API performs every universal delivery as a style sent with each sentence, added to the `prompt` setting's style, with emphasis in capitals and inline sounds and pauses such as `<laugh>` and `<short pause>`.
  - `AzureTTSService` and `AzureHttpTTSService` with a DragonHD or DragonHD Omni voice, such as `en-US-Ava:DragonHDOmniLatestNeural`, perform a delivery as one of Azure's HD styles, such as `[excited]`, and its paralinguistic sounds, such as `[laughter]`.
  - `ExpressionDelivery` and `ExpressionSound` gain the universal names these services perform, such as `playful`, `soft`, `encouraging`, `sniffle` and `whimper`.
