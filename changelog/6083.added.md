- Added `DidlogicFrameSerializer`, which carries a Pipecat bot's audio over a DIDLogic WebSocket call — inbound to one of your numbers, or the agent leg of an outbound Click2Call placed with `a_type: "stream"`. Audio is PCM16 24 kHz mono, base64 inside JSON messages. DTMF arrives as `InputDTMFFrame`; interruptions and hang-ups go out as the protocol's `clear` and `hangup`, the latter needing no credentials since it is one more message on the open socket. `answered` and `stop` reach the pipeline as `InputTransportMessageFrame`s, which is what lets an outbound bot hold its greeting until the destination picks up — DIDLogic answers the bot's leg before the phone rings, so anything said earlier plays into ringback. `-t didlogic` works in the development runner, and `DidlogicCallData` types the `direction` field the handshake carries.
