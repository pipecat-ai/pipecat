- `pipecat eval` can drive a bot over a WebSocket the bot accepted itself, such as a Pipecat Cloud session or the dev runner's `/ws` route. The harness sends its connection's flags in an `eval-connect` message ahead of the RTVI handshake, and `create_transport` builds the new `EvalFastAPIWebsocketTransport` from it, so a bot's existing `transport_params["eval"]` entry works unchanged.
