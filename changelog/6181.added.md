- Added `AICTytoObserver`, which scores the quality of the user's audio with the ai-coustics Tyto model after every 5 seconds of received audio. Each result fires the `on_analysis_result` event with the risk score and per-degradation scores. At the end of the call, `on_call_summary` receives the mean, p95, and max scores, the degraded fraction, and the dominant degradation dimension. Pass the transport's `AICFilter` as `audio_filter` to score the audio before enhancement.
