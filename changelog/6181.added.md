- Added `AICTytoProcessor`, which scores the quality of the user's audio with the ai-coustics Tyto model after every 5 seconds of received audio. Each result fires the `on_analysis_result` event, is pushed as a new `AudioQualityFrame` (risk score, per-degradation scores, and `audio_secs`), and is reported as `MetricsFrame`s. At the end of the call, `on_call_summary` receives the mean, p95, and max risk, the degraded fraction, and the dominant degradation dimension. `RTVIObserver` includes the results in its metrics message under the new `audio_quality` and `audio_quality_summary` keys.
