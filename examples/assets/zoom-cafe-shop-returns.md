# Zoom Cafe Shop returns knowledge base

The example store's return policy, refunds and FAQ, one topic per `##` section. `realtime-zoom-ai-decisions.py` searches these sections.

## Return window

You have 30 days from delivery to send most items back for a return. The 30 days start on the delivery date shown in your order history, not the order date. After 30 days we can't accept change-of-mind returns, but damaged or defective items may still be covered by our warranty.

## Items that can't be returned

Gift cards, e-gift cards and other final-sale items can't be returned or exchanged. Items marked "final sale" on the product page and in your order confirmation are not eligible for a refund. Perishable food, such as coffee beans that have been opened, can't be returned for hygiene reasons.

## Changed your mind

If you no longer want an item, you can return it within 30 days for a full refund to your original payment method. The item should be unused and in its original packaging. We email a prepaid return label; there's no restocking fee. The refund is issued once the item arrives at our warehouse.

## Wrong item or not as described

If we sent the wrong item, size or color, or the item doesn't match its description, we'll exchange it for the right one or give you a full refund. Return shipping is always free in this case, and we can send the replacement before the wrong item comes back.

## Damaged or defective items

If an item arrives broken, cracked, chipped, dented or doesn't work, contact us within 30 days of delivery and describe the damage. We refund damaged items in full, and you don't need to send them back; please recycle them.

## When a person reviews your case

If we can't tell from your description what's wrong with the item, or the claim falls outside these rules, a returns specialist reviews the case and emails you within one business day. We never deny a damage claim automatically.

## How long refunds take

Refunds go back to your original payment method. Once a refund is issued, card refunds usually appear within 5 to 7 business days, depending on your bank. PayPal refunds appear within 2 business days. Store-credit refunds are available immediately.

## Replacements

Free replacements ship within 2 business days by standard shipping, at no cost to you. If the item is out of stock, we'll offer a full refund instead.

## Return labels and drop-off

After a return is approved, we email a prepaid UPS return label within a few minutes. Print it, attach it to the box, and drop it at any UPS location within 14 days. You can track the return from the link in the email.

## Exchanges

We exchange items for a different size or color of the same product. To switch to a different product, return the item for a refund and place a new order.

## Shipping times

Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days and is free on orders over $35. Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days and costs $12.

## Warranty

Electronics, such as earbuds and grinders, carry a 1-year limited warranty from the delivery date. After the 30-day return window, warranty claims for defects are handled by our warranty team, who reply by email within 2 business days.

## Can I return a gift?

Yes. Gift recipients can return gifted items within 30 days of delivery for store credit, without the original payment details. The person who bought the gift isn't notified. Gift cards themselves are final sale and can't be returned.

## Do I need the original packaging?

For change-of-mind returns, please use the original packaging if you still have it; any sturdy box is fine. For damaged items you don't need any packaging, because you don't send them back.

## Can I cancel an order?

You can cancel within 1 hour of placing the order from your order history. After that, the order has usually shipped, and you can return it once it arrives.

## Do you ship internationally?

We currently ship only within the United States, so international returns don't apply.

## How do I contact a person?

Ask the returns assistant to connect you, or email support@zoomcafeshop.example. A returns specialist replies within one business day.
